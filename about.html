Nikolai Grigoriev

I make a life visible so that it can be lived as a work of art — and the life is my own. A body records itself without being asked; day after day those traces — how I rested, how I slept, the evenness of my breath — pass into the painting by one unchanging rule, and it changes as I change. Nothing is arranged by hand. The work and my life are made together: as long as I keep living, it keeps being painted.

So my material is living itself — I aestheticize my life, not my death; death only stands behind me, sharpening how much a day is worth once you know it ends. The work never shows me the present: a day becomes an image only after it has closed, when there is nothing left to perform for. When the life falls silent the painting greys and stills; when the days resume, it returns. I came to art late and built its language myself. I do not want to leave objects — I want to leave a way of living.

Biography

Nikolai Grigoriev (b. 1987, Moscow) is a conceptual artist living and working in Barcelona. His work turns the daily record of a single living body — his own — into painting: one image a day, since 2022, made by one unchanging rule that never repaints the past. The work changes as he changes and stills when the life falls silent; he came to art late, and built its language himself.
