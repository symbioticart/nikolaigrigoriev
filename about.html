Nikolai Grigoriev

Nikolai Grigoriev makes paintings from the record his own body keeps. Each day, a ring reads his sleep, his pulse, the evenness of his breath, and one unchanging rule turns that day's body-state into a single painting. He writes the rule; his life supplies the data. Work and life evolve together, day after day — nothing is composed by hand.

The work never shows the present: a day becomes an image only once it has closed, when it can no longer be performed for. When the body falls silent, the painting greys and stills — the record can die, and, when the signal returns, live again. What medicine cannot reach in a life, art can — its felt time. He slows it, keeps every day, and hands the rule on to outlast the body. He came to art late, and means to leave not objects but a way of living.

Biography

Nikolai Grigoriev (b. 1987, Moscow) is a conceptual artist. He lives and works in Barcelona.
