Nikolai Grigoriev

Nikolai Grigoriev makes paintings from the quiet record his own life keeps. Each day — its rest and its unrest, the depth of its nights, the evenness of its breath — passes through one unchanging rule and settles into a single painting. He sets the rule; the living does the rest. Work and life move together, day after day, and nothing is arranged by hand.

The work never shows the present: a day becomes an image only once it has closed, when nothing in it can still be performed for. When the life falls silent, the painting greys and stills — it can die, and, when the days resume, return. What medicine cannot reach in a life, art can — its felt time. He slows it, keeps every day, and hands the rule on to outlast the body. He came to art late, and means to leave not objects but a way of living.

Biography

Nikolai Grigoriev (b. 1987, Moscow) is a conceptual artist. He lives and works in Barcelona.
