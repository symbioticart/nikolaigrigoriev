Nikolai Grigoriev

Conditions of the Work

S1-01 · 2022–Today · this document constitutes the work

1The Work

“S1-01” (until September 2026 shown as “Variation 87”) is a living work by Nikolai Grigoriev. It turns a single life, the traces one body leaves without being asked, into an image, one day at a time, so that living can be seen and lived as a work of art. What death sharpens is attention: a life is worth this much once you know it ends. A dated state is written “S1-01, state of [date].” The work is one; its daily states are states of one work. Medium: software, lived time written daily by an unchanging rule, screen.

2Definition

The rule and this certificate constitute the work. Any image, screen or print is an execution of the rule: evidence of the work, not the work itself. Whoever holds this certificate holds the work. Without it there is no work.

3The Rule

Each day, the daily signal of one living body is turned into one painting. The seed of a day is drawn from the name of the artist, the date and the day’s record together, by a fixed formula. Every day is weighed only against the days of this body’s own history that precede it; a hard day is hard by its own measure. The signals are entangled before they reach the canvas: every visible feature is driven by several at once, and no measurement can be read back out of the image. The same day always produces the same sequence of marks. Once a day has ended its inputs never change, and its painting never changes. The work never shows the present. A day is met only once it has closed and can no longer be performed for. The delay removes the audience. What remains is presence.

4The Signal

The work accepts the daily signal of a living body from any instrument able to provide the schema of Appendix A. It is bound to no maker and no device. The artist is the sole subject of the signal in this work, by his own consent. Keeping the signal flowing is the duty of its subject. An interruption is not a breach. It is silence, and silence is part of the work. Appendix A, the exact schema of the daily signal, is printed only with the signed certificate and appears on no public surface of the work.

5Silence

Mortality is the edge of the work, the pressure that gives a life its attention. When no new day arrives, the work does not pretend. The last painting is held. From the second silent day its colour begins to leave. By the fourteenth the image stands without colour, and it goes on dissolving into the empty ground until, by the thirtieth silent day, nothing remains but the ground. The work is then empty, not finished. Any day that arrives restores it in full at once; the work returns as many times as the days do, and no silence is final. Silence is never declared from a stored record alone, only by the living programme itself, so a sleeping machine cannot show a false death and an instrument that has stopped is never painted as a body that has. Upon the certified death of the signal’s subject the last state is permanent and the work is complete. The artist’s obligations do not survive the artist.

6Withdrawal

The subject of the signal may end his participation at any time. A declared withdrawal, a signed and dated statement recorded in the provenance log, is written as silence from its date. The work registers absence, never its cause, and an undeclared silence is never named a death. Withdrawal acts forward only: the days already written remain part of the work.

7The Archive

The work keeps every day it has lived. Each state is frozen as it was written on its own day; the future never repaints the past. Recorded gaps remain visible as gaps. The archive is the work’s memory and its full body.

8Transfer

This certificate transfers the work; re-registration of this document is the transfer. The bond to the artist’s body survives any sale: this work is not re-bound to another body. If the certificate is lost, the artist may reissue it once, in person, recorded in the provenance log. After the artist there is no reissue.

9Care

The artist maintains the performing programme and one working signal adapter for five years from the date of transfer. After that the owner holds the complete rule, the code, the seed formula and the schema of Appendix A: enough for any competent technician to perform any state of the work again.
