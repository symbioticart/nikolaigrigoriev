The Rule

Variations 87 · Variation 89
  1. Each day, one painting.
  2. The painting is made from that day’s record of one living body, and from nothing else.
  3. Nothing is arranged by hand.
  4. A day is painted only after it has closed.
  5. A day is weighed against the days of this body that came before it, never against the days that follow.
  6. Several signals drive every visible feature, so that no single one can be read back out.
  7. The same day always makes the same painting.
  8. A day that has been painted is never repainted.
  9. When no day arrives, the last painting is held. From the second day of silence it fades.
  10. When the days resume, the painting returns.
  11. Every day the work has lived is kept.
Both works follow this rule. First day 24 May 2022.
The full conditions of Variations 87 — its definition, its silence, its transfer — are set out in the document that constitutes it: Conditions of the Work.
